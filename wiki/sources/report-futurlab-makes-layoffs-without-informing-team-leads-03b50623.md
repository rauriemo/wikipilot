## Excerpts

> FuturLab, the studio behind PowerWash Simulator, laid off seven employees following the cancellation of a project that had been in development for between two to three years.

> The studio recently canceled a new non-PowerWash related project while in pre-production, as it would have cost us more than it was projected to make.

> Managers and team leads were not informed that their direct reports were going to be away or why.

> Despite the layoffs, work will continue on PowerWash Simulator 2, as new updates and DLC content continue to be deployed.
