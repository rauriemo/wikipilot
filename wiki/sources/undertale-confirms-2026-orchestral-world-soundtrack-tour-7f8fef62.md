## Excerpts

> Undertale: The Determination Symphony is a live orchestral concert tour celebrating the 10th anniversary of Toby Fox's beloved 2015 RPG, featuring a 25-piece orchestra performing new arrangements of the Undertale soundtrack, accompanied by gameplay footage.

> The tour runs from August 23, 2026 in San Jose, California through March 12, 2027 in Lima, Peru, spanning over 70 shows across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. Music has been orchestrated by British orchestrator Adam Hoskins.

> The concert takes gig-goers on an emotional musical journey through the Underground, from your first steps among the Ruins to the final moments of your adventure — from Snowdin to Waterfall, Hotland, and finally New Home, bringing iconic characters, battles, and emotional moments vividly to life through music.
