## Excerpts

> Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 – A Painted Symphony has announced 24 new dates for 2027 and 2028, expanding the orchestral world tour of Lorien Testard and Alice Duport-Percier's celebrated score across Europe and North America with all-new scenography created especially for the stage.

> The new 2027 leg will feature Lorien Testard, Alice Duport-Percier, and Orchestre Curieux performing the adventure's iconic themes in a spectacular live musical production. Venues confirmed include The O2 in London (February 28, 2027), Uber Arena in Berlin (April 1), Unity Arena in Oslo (April 27), Avicii Arena in Stockholm (April 28), and Radio City Music Hall in New York City on September 29 and 30, 2027.

> Tickets for the newly announced 2027 dates go on general sale on September 15, 2026, at 4 PM BST via apaintedsymphony.expedition33.com.
