> The AI Gaming Festival has opened on Steam, running from September 7 to September 28, 2026. This is a third-party event presented through a Steam curator page, not one of Valve's own themed festivals.

> Its stated goal is to educate players and developers about how AI is being used in game creation while showcasing projects built with the technology.

> The third-party Steam AI Gaming Festival is live until September 28, but unexplained entries include Bad Rats across all 12 AI-use categories. An online conference hosted through Glitch runs from September 10 to September 30.
