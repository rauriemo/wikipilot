## Excerpts

> Blue Reflection: Second Light takes what made the original game's music so strong and develops it with more experimentation and variety whilst also revisiting some of the compositions from the first game.

> The soundtrack starts off very strong with the track 'Glitter'—an interesting combination of breathy vocals and a modern take on city pop and jazz.

> The soundtrack brings back composer Hayato Asano and features new collaborator Toshihiko Watanabe.

> The main complaint with the OST is an abundance of overly long tracks, with many four-minute-plus tracks that do not evolve much over the duration and lose their impact.

> The mood is very much one of positivity, lightness and reminiscence—you'd listen to it while taking it easy on a summer evening, reflecting on past memories and enjoying some calm space to yourself.
