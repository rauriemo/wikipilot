## Excerpts

> Olivier Derivière's score for Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy features The Amalgamation Choir, a unique Greek-Cypriot ensemble of 20 female vocalists led by Vasiliki Anastasiou, alongside Cypriot soloists and traditional instruments including Yaylı tambur, rebab, carnyx, bouzouki, and cello. There are between six and ten hours of music in the game.

> Writing the songs was particularly challenging, as the lyrics translated from French into the Cypriot language — Derivière noted that using the native language of the singers made a significant difference in the emotional impact of the music.

> The entire soundtrack is conceived as a musical journey moving between an intimate human story and a mystical enigma, with the OST structure mirroring the game's own narrative structure from beginning to end.
