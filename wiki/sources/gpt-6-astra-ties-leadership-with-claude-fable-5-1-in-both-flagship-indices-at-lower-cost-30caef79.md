## Excerpts

> GPT-6 Astra ties leadership with Claude Fable 5.1 in both of our flagship Indices, at lower cost. Astra equals Fable 5.1 in the Intelligence Index at ~40% of the cost, and in the Coding Agent Index at ~60% of the cost.

> In Codex, GPT-6 Astra scores 62 in the Index, level with Claude Fable 5.1 in Claude Code (62) and ahead of Claude Opus 5 (60), GPT-5.6 Sol (55) and Muse Spark 1.3 in Muse Code (54).

> GPT-6 Astra ties Claude Fable 5.1 at ~60% of the cost per task, driven by the lowest token use of any agent in the Index. At max effort it costs $7.09 per task, ~15% more than GPT-5.6 Sol (max) for a 7 point higher score, and ~40% less than Claude Fable 5.1 (max with fallback) in Claude Code for the same score.

> GPT-6 Astra's 7 point lead over GPT-5.6 Sol comes from Terminal-Bench v4.0 (56% vs 37%) and SWE-Atlas-QnA (62% vs 54%), partly offset by a lower score on DeepSWE (68% vs 72%).

> The Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index is a composite score built from DeepSWE v1.1, Terminal-Bench 4.0, and SWE-Atlas-QnA.
