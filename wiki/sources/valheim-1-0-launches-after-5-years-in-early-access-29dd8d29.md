## Excerpts

> Valheim 1.0 officially launches on September 9 on all platforms, including Windows, Linux, macOS, the Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2.

> The Deep North is the centerpiece of Valheim 1.0 and the game's final biome update, featuring a frozen region with new environmental hazards, resources, enemies, recipes, and other content to discover.

> Valheim 1.0 includes new weapons, new traversal options, a new dungeon type, and a new mount, among others, and makes cross-play available for all platforms.

> The price rises from 19.99 dollars to 29.99 dollars; existing saves transfer, but mods may require updates.
