## Excerpts

> 505 Games and Laced Records are presenting 29 key tracks from Leenzee Games' 2025 action RPG Wuchang: Fallen Feathers on a double vinyl set, featuring music composed by Xu Wutong, Zhai Jinyan, Zhang Zhenkun, and Cao Lishu, all specially mastered for vinyl by Joe Caithness.

> The 2LP is available in two vinyl color variants: an exclusive black-in-red translucent colored vinyl, and a standard black vinyl variant. Both editions come in a gatefold sleeve adorned with artwork by developer Leenzee depicting protagonist Bai Wuchang. Pre-orders are priced at $42 or $50 depending on region, with all orders dispatched on September 18, 2026.
