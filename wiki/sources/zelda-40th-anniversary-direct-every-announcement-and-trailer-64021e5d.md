## Excerpts

> Nintendo announced a release date for the Switch 2 Ocarina of Time remake during The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct on September 8, 2026. The exact date is November 5, 2026.

> The Legend of Zelda movie will officially be called 'The Legend of Zelda'. The movie will be releasing in theaters on April 30, 2027.

> A special Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Edition console will be available starting on October 29 ahead of Ocarina of Time's release.

> The remake features rebuilt visuals, new controls, voiced cutscenes, expanded dialogue, and several gameplay changes.

> A Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert will be held in 2027 throughout Japan, North America, Europe, and Australia.
