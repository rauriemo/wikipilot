## Excerpts

> Nintendo will be sharing non-game related initiatives for The Legend of Zelda's 40th anniversary, as well as details about the Nintendo Switch 2 software 'The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time'.

> The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct starts on September 8 at 7am PT, with around 30 minutes dedicated to the legendary series. A second Nintendo Direct follows on September 9, running around 45 minutes and focusing on Switch 2 games arriving this winter.

> The Ocarina of Time remake for the Switch 2 is currently scheduled to launch in 2026, so a release date announcement seems likely.
