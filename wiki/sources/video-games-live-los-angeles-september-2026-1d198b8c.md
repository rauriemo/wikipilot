## Excerpts

> Video Games Live returns to Los Angeles for two performances with the San Gabriel Valley Symphony at the historic Mission Playhouse in San Gabriel on September 11 and 12, 2026 at 8:00 PM. The all-new 2026 setlist will feature music from Halo, Final Fantasy, Tetris, Warcraft, Skyrim, Kingdom Hearts, The Last of Us, and Undertale, plus Video Games Live world premieres from Minecraft, Studio Ghibli, and Expedition 33.

> For the first time ever, Video Games Live will add anime music to its official setlist. Special composer and conductor guests will appear from around the world. Tickets are priced at $43–$84.
